4. We agreed to proceed with 2 methods:
  **A**. Stellar population: inside the milky way, age and [Fe/H] follows a trend, and clusters with more iron tend to be a bit younger. A cluster born in a small galaxy grew its metals more slowly, so at the same [Fe/H] it is often younger than the Milky Way trend.
  **B**. Dynamics: the milky way's GC system as a whole rotates slowly, with a lot of random motion. A cluster moving very differently from that system is suspicious, because accreted clusters keep the orbit of the galaxy they came from.
